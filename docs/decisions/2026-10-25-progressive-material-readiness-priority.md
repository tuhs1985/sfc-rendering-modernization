# Sponsor priority — fast complete rendering, progressive materials as fallback

Approved2026-10-25 after Run177. Primary objective remains a fast, fully prepared
returned scene and the existing sub-two-second loading target. Earlier native
data is useful but does not excuse multi-second downstream preparation.

If complete enhanced-material readiness cannot be immediate, sponsor prefers the
correct ship appearing promptly with original diffuse textures/basic material
response, followed by enhanced materials becoming ready, rather than no returned
ship until the final appearance is prepared. This authorizes evaluation of
progressive material readiness as a secondary strategy, not a claim that the full
loading target is met or permission to hide an expensive stage behind a new delay.

Run163's fast first pixels lacked enhanced material response but retained ordinary
diffuse textures. That remains a failed full-appearance parity result; it may now
be useful as an explicitly provisional first image. Do not relabel historical
results or enable the one-Present control in production without validating its
delivery/appearance transition and existing safety requirements.

Correct geometry/identity, current camera/pose, original image associations,
bounded output and original-game behavior remain required. No wrong-ship,
untextured substitute, stale frame or unsafe original suppression is authorized.
Original-ship replacement and its visibility/synchronization gates remain separate.
Lighting/material/image settings unchanged; API remains parked. No CDD threshold
or milestone gate is amended by this delivery preference.

Exact next action: inspect existing accepted D3D9 delivery/first-correct-output
records and supported material-consumption path; attribute the largest delay
before choosing a narrowly tested implementation. No new gameplay discovery run.
