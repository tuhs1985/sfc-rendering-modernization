# M1-A-RT-002 — Multi-Frame Semantic Lifetime and Stability

## Technical question and hypothesis

Can component version 0.2 preserve rich semantic owner/model/source/lifetime
evidence across a useful tactical window while counting every `0x006AB410` call
but retaining only bounded per-owner/per-frame geometry samples?

**Hypothesis:** two ship-owner chains remain coherent during camera/object
movement; temporarily removing one ship from view retires its owner epoch after
the existing two-frame absence rule; reappearance creates a new opaque epoch
without cross-association; sampled geometry remains sufficient to demonstrate
continued downstream correlation.

## Authority and targets

- Approved M1 plan, M1-05.
- `M1-A-CONTRACT-001`; hook addresses and ABI are unchanged from the confirmed
  neutral pass-through.
- Component/schema: `0.2` / `m1.semantic.v1`.
- Exact client MD5: `2BE8F713F81B1C68D50EAD6088C32886`.

## Capture strategy and bounds

- Count every correlated and uncorrelated `0x006AB410` call.
- Retain at most one correlated geometry record per owner per scene epoch and
  one uncorrelated geometry record per scene epoch.
- Retain ship, visual, Model0, source/selected-child, visual-exit, scene, and
  retirement events until the overall bound.
- Maximum 6,000 scene epochs, 100,000 retained events, eight owners, 90 seconds.
- Event-log warning/hard thresholds remain 16/32 MiB.
- Expected event log: 15–25 MiB; expected total comfortably below 32 MiB and far
  below the sponsor's 10 GB absolute temporary ceiling.
- Stop on the first frame/event/time bound; thereafter restore all hooks and
  preserve original rendering.

The larger frame/event envelope is justified because the confirmed earlier
10,000-event run was consumed by geometry in one frame. Sampling removes that
firehose; the enlarged envelope is needed to observe real-time absence and
reappearance without relaxing the existing file-size stop.

## Launch and sponsor actions

Run the scoped elevated workflow with tactical arming:

```powershell
pwsh -NoProfile -File tools/m1_component/start_m1_enabled_session.ps1 `
  -MaxSeconds 90 -MaxFrames 6000 -MaxEvents 100000 `
  -GeometrySamplesPerOwnerFrame 1 `
  -UncorrelatedGeometrySamplesPerFrame 1
```

After the sponsor reports tactical entry, create the documented arm signal.
Sponsor actions during the bounded window:

1. keep both ships visible briefly;
2. move/rotate a ship and move the camera;
3. frame one ship so the other is outside the rendered view for several seconds;
4. return both ships to view and continue normal selection/targeting;
5. after confirmed restoration, exit normally and report any visible issue.

If the game continues invoking both owners while off-screen, absence cannot be
induced by camera movement; record that as an engine visibility result rather
than forcing a broader scenario.

## Acceptance and analysis

- At least two coherent owner chains across multiple scene epochs.
- No ship/visual/model/source cross-association or vtable mismatch.
- Bounded sampled geometry for each mainline owner plus complete total counters.
- Retirement and reappearance with a fresh opaque ID if the fixture induces
  absence; otherwise explicitly classify that condition as unobserved.
- Known `0x00509843` exception remains finite and unassociated by guess.
- No semantic rejection, overflow, truncation, restoration failure, visible
  regression, abnormal exit, client-hash change, or residual game file.

Raw output is analyzed in place, then may be compressed or removed after curated
evidence is recorded. Unrestricted geometry transcripts remain prohibited.

## Runtime result — 2026-09-05

Accepted run `20260905-101749-enabled` observed 6,000 scene epochs over about 56
seconds. The component counted 32,409,660 calls at `0x006AB410` while retaining
only 6,000 correlated and 6,001 uncorrelated geometry samples. The retained CSV
was 17,393,499 bytes; the complete run was 17,417,214 bytes, below the 32 MiB
hard event-log limit.

Two original ship pointers produced four coherent opaque epochs. Owner 1 retired
at frame 2,469 and the same ship/visual/model/source chain reappeared as owner 3
from frame 2,585 through frame 5,999. Owner 2 retired at frame 3,607, reappeared
as owner 4 at frame 4,289, and retired again at frame 5,393. Each epoch retained
one ship, visual, Model0, and source identity; no cross-association, vtable
mismatch, rejected semantic event, owner/thread overflow, or log truncation was
observed. Movement and camera changes did not disturb these associations. The
probe does not claim a decoded numeric transform snapshot; it confirms stable
semantic/manager handoff identity while the sponsor exercised motion.

The known `0x00509843` path occurred 6,001 times, approximately once per scene
epoch. It remained finite, deliberately unassigned to a ship, and did not
invalidate the mainline correlation, but is now characterized as systematic
rather than rare in this fixture.

All six hooks restored and the controller independently verified restoration.
The process exited normally and exact client hashes remained unchanged. No
game-directory loader/configuration remained. The sponsor reported one
3–5-second microstutter near the beginning and no other visible rendering, HUD,
cursor, targeting, camera, movement, or control issue.

The runtime lifetime evidence is **CONFIRMED**, but behavioral equivalence is not
yet confirmed. With 32.4 million geometry-hook entries, hot-path instrumentation
cost is a **STRONG HYPOTHESIS** for the microstutter; this run alone cannot
separate it from ordinary tactical-entry loading. Decision: **M1 CONTINUES —
additional bounded evidence required**. The next probe should attribute the
startup stutter and reduce hot-path work, not increase output volume.

The component summary reported zero retirement events although the retained CSV
contained and the analyzer verified three. Synthetic retirement records bypassed
the hook-level counter. The post-run source corrects that summary counter for
future runs; the defect does not alter the retained lifetime evidence.
