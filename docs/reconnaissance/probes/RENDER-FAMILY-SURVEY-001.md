## Observation boundary and exclusions

Separate default-off observer, pinned full-support ReShade6.7.1/API18 on the
verified D3D11 presentation chain. Reuse existing startup-path verification,
private channel, automatic tactical gate and verified proxy rollback. No Remix
or Toolkit process, SDK scene submission, extra ship or image-return rendering.

Observe lifecycle, draws/counts, target/view/resource/buffer identities, texture
and sampler bindings, pipeline/dynamic state and bounded call-origin stacks.
Keep all submitted categories, including unknowns and ship DRAW metadata. Do not
discard a draw merely because it appears ship-like. Never read vertex/index/UV/
normal arrays, initial resource bytes, shader bytecode, image pixels, mapped
buffers or on-disk assets. This excludes known bulk payloads, not unknown paths.

Call origins use Windows CaptureStackBackTrace with finite depth and sampling,
not symbols or stack scanning in the callback. Record loss/empty/truncated or
unassociated origins. Module ranges collected outside callbacks permit offline
address-to-module joins. A translator's asynchronous/deferred path may hide the
original SFC call; missing engine origins are unknowns, not guessed owners.
